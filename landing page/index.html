WHO IT'S FOR

Who are you planning heart surgery for?

Your parent moved here on a green card. Their heart care shouldn't wait on Medicare.

Parents in their first five years in the US can't get Medicare, and many low-income parents lost subsidised Marketplace plans in January 2026. Those with few US work years can pay about $9,200 a year just to buy into Medicare.

HeartBridge lets your parent have surgery in India, close to family and in their own language, while a US cardiologist plans it with the Indian surgeon and takes over care when they return.

Usually no visa needed

An Indian passport or OCI card covers entry. We check this at sign-up.

A dashboard you control

Their language

Green card kept safe

WHY NOT GO ALONE

The surgery is rarely the problem. The gaps between two countries are.

India has world-class cardiac centres. What goes wrong is everything around them.

Records

US records can take up to 30 days to release, and heart imaging often arrives on a CD.

Prices

Indian package prices aren't binding, and agent commissions of 20–30% push bills up.

Paperwork

A medical visa needs a hospital invitation letter first, and you can't fly for about 10 days after chest surgery.

Coming home

Patients often return without records, implant details or a US doctor ready to see them.

FAQ

Questions families ask

Does Medicare pay for surgery in India?

No. Medicare doesn't pay for planned care abroad, so the India procedure is self-pay. If your parent has Medicare, their US visits before and after surgery are billed as usual.

Who can't use HeartBridge?

Anyone with an acute or emergency heart problem. If you have chest pain now, call 911. We work with stable conditions that can be planned.

How long will we be in India?

Usually three to four weeks for a bypass. Doctors advise no flying for about 10 days after chest surgery, and we won't book the flight home until you're cleared.

Who sees my medical information?

Only the doctors and hospital on your case, and family members you choose. You consent separately for each, and can set clinical, travel and payment access per person.
